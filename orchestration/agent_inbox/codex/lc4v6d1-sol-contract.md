## Diagnostic question

Practitioner text recognition and practitioner-directory resolution are
different layers. Context-free extraction may losslessly recognize `Dr Rivera`
as an exact practitioner mention without knowing whether that name maps to an
authoritative practitioner ID. Option A policy owns that roster lookup and must
fail closed with clarification, no mutation tool, no deltas, and no simulated
write when the ID is unknown.

Therefore extraction and policy clarification are deliberately expected to
differ for the unknown-practitioner family. A runner that requires identical
clarification state at both layers is a contract-granularity defect, not proof
of a parser failure. The diagnostic must score the two layers separately.
