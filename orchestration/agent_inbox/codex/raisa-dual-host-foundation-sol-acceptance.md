# Sol acceptance — Raisa candidate dual-host foundation

Date: 2026-07-31

Disposition: `accepted`

The increment satisfies its provider-free acceptance contract:

- the host profile is pure, closed, deeply immutable and grants no authority;
- authored desktop, web, mobile and unknown fixtures pass;
- source and published runtimes are byte-identical and load before the taskpane;
- the existing clinician and Reception One surfaces are inventoried without
  reclassifying historical evidence or activating legacy clinical paths;
- Reception One is durably recorded as one backend-owned domain with
  role-scoped staff, doctor and future online-booking and Rayleen surfaces;
- cloud-first practice management as a service is recorded as the primary
  delivery direction, with any future local model restricted to a subordinate
  edge rather than a parallel source of truth;
- future patient surfaces and third-party adapters receive no present
  authority;
- focused Word, Reception One and API Spine tests and the development build
  pass; and
- task-owned runtime residue is absent.

The acceptance is deliberately narrow. It is not evidence of authenticated
Word Online operation, microphone or scribe behavior, backend/provider access,
patient-facing behavior, production fitness, public branding or release.
