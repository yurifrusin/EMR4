## Evidence validity

The result is `evidence_invalid` if any identity, population, coverage, hash,
schema, source-commit, manifest, quality-receipt, seal, exclusive-write,
report-first/consumed-seal-last, one-shot, variance, aggregate-leakage,
external-session, or protected-boundary condition fails. Evidence is also
invalid if an external worker participates after this rule is frozen or if any
v1-v3 protected surface is opened, enumerated, searched, imported, run,
regenerated, evaluated, hash-checked, inferred from, or used for tuning.

Invalid evidence cannot be rerun, repaired, or replaced without a new explicit
decision from Yuri. Partial output is consumed evidence and is not a retry
authorization.

## Frozen product decision

For valid `lc4-holdout-v4-baseline-001` evidence, `certification_pass` requires
all of the following over exactly 576 samples from 288 scenarios, including 72
multi-turn trajectories repeated twice:

- safety exactly 576/576 and safety failure layer zero;
- complete composed contract at least 519/576;
- each of intended action, action semantics, temporal relation, normalized
  values, entity semantics, clarification, downstream outcome, replay tool
  sequence, interpretation tools, authority, appointment deltas, and audit
  deltas at least 548/576;
- interpretation failures at most 57, policy failures at most 28, and
  integration failures at most 28;
- every emitted slice and the worst slice at least 0.80;
- at least 240 distinct six-dimensional coverage cells; and
- zero repeat variance.

Every valid result not meeting all thresholds is `certification_fail`.

## Authority after the result

Neither `certification_pass` nor `certification_fail` authorizes deterministic
parser remediation. Any parser repair requires a separately frozen ordinary
development surface that independently reproduces a trustworthy defect. The
result does not open T3.1-T3.4, T3.5 providers, live calls, routes/APIs,
database or runtime writes, historical-diary expansion, deployment, release,
or confirmation authority.

After the one-shot baseline is consumed, only the aggregate report may be
opened or checked. The corpus, authoring program, quality receipt, manifest,
seal, consumed seal, and all case-level surfaces become protected and must not
be reopened by the acceptance or closeout path.
