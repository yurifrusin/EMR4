# LC4V3 Sol Fresh Certification Holdout Contract

## Authorization and purpose

Yuri authorized a genuinely fresh certification holdout on 2026-07-15 after
LC4V2E1 returned `no_r3_authorized`. LC4V3 measures the accepted deterministic
language bridge without reusing or inspecting either sealed predecessor.

LC4V3 is a certification procedure, not a parser-repair tranche and not live
T3.5 execution. Its result may pass or fail product readiness. No repair may be
made from hidden case content after evaluation.

## Protected predecessor boundary

Protected holdouts v1 and v2 remain sealed. No participant may open,
enumerate, list, search, import, run, regenerate, evaluate, hash-check, infer
from, or tune against their fixtures, support modules, authoring surfaces,
manifests, seals, receipts, per-case reports, or filename populations.

Planning may use only the committed aggregate v2 closeout, the content-blind
procedure description, the accepted development aggregates, and LC4V2E1's
repair-exit decision. No predecessor case, label, utterance, source span,
filename, or per-slice case allocation may be recovered.

1. DeepSeek V4 Flash/high through Claude Code `--bare` may implement only the
   content-blind framework and synthetic miniature tests. No real LC4V3 case
   may exist during its work.
2. Sol reviews and accepts or recovers the framework under the Ariadne recovery

5. The frozen corpus is committed before the one-shot baseline. The baseline
   runs exactly once, emits aggregate-only evidence, marks the seal consumed,
   and immediately closes all LC4V3 content surfaces.

No external worker may see actual LC4V3 content, certify its own framework,
integrate, advance the baton, or push protected refs.

## Content-blind framework contract

The framework must support exactly:

- 24 groups named `lc4v3_group_001` through `lc4v3_group_024`;
- 12 Gold/adjudicated `ReceptionScenarioSpec` variants per group;
- three real multi-turn trajectories per group;
- 288 total scenarios, 72 trajectories, and two deterministic repeats;
- exactly 576 production aggregate samples;
- fixed evaluation ID `lc4-holdout-v3-baseline-001`;
- evaluator version `lc4v3.aggregate_evaluator.v1`; and
- a distinct future corpus identity `lc4-holdout-v3`.

The manifest binds sorted safe relative filenames, individual file hashes,
schema/version, case counts, and a canonical corpus hash. Seal creation must
reconstruct and exactly verify the manifest, then bind the full source commit,
manifest and corpus hashes, evaluator version, evaluation ID, and repeat
policy.

Every future scenario must use explicit synthetic diary state, a unique
namespaced ID, an explicit expected outcome field, and non-empty lossless
source-span evidence. Expected labels and source-span names must never enter
interpretation or replay.

Production evaluation must use only the public deterministic interpretation,
replay, and composed-scoring path. It keeps case observations in process and
emits only fixed aggregate dimension totals, failure-layer totals, variance,
predeclared slice totals, coverage-cell counts, scenario/sample totals, safe
identity hashes, and the canonical report hash. Recursive forbidden-key lint
must reject utterances, IDs, expected labels, tools, deltas, source spans,
normalized values, observations, findings, or other case-level structures.

The CLI exposes explicit pre-content authoring commands, `create-seal`,
`baseline-once`, and post-consumption `check-aggregate`. Mutating commands
require `--write`. `baseline-once` refuses if either authority-bearing output
already exists, writes the report exclusively, and writes the consumed seal
last. After consumption, no check may load or hash the protected corpus.
