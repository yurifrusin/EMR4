repository-owned mechanics are no longer represented as open prose or repeated
arrays at the control surface. One selector replaces 36 command leaves; one
selector replaces six Baton-acceptance leaves; and one timestamp derives ten
human-evidence values. The 61-leaf live request retains semantic judgment:
authority notes, decisions, claims, unresolved gates, present meaning and the
next operation.

coordinates private. The useful worker boundary remains `prepared`,
`terminal`, and `accepted_or_recovered`; the clock does not attempt per-turn
or per-tool synchronization with a worker harness.
