# Reception One holistic Bureau direction

Date: 2026-07-29

Owner: Yuri / GPT Sol

Decision: `accepted_product_direction_external_staff_study_paused`

## Why the direction changed

Yuri reviewed the Stage 3B study surface and concluded that Reception One is
not yet ready for independent receptionist evaluation. The current instrument
puts too much participant attention on synthetic-data provenance and
governance language, while the product still does not demonstrate the
end-to-end natural-language experience that is meant to distinguish it from an
ordinary Diary grid.

The accepted Stage 3B readiness result remains valid historical evidence that
its frozen provider-free protocol can run safely. It is not revised or
relabelled. External participant execution is paused, however, and is no longer
the current next action.

The immediate evidence sequence is:

1. a Yuri-only internal walkthrough of the present authored-synthetic product;
2. an integrated provider-free Bureau shell and projection choreography;
3. a separately authorised bounded natural-language model lane over
   authored-synthetic text;
4. a design-partner session, initially suitable for the former Flinders Peak
   practice manager; and
5. only after revision, a broader representative receptionist study.

## One ordinary Diary, one foreground projection

The conventional Diary remains a first-class, visible spatial reference and
escape route. Reception One should not replace it with an unrelated page.

On desktop and tablet, an intent projection should appear as a modeless,
window-like layer in front of the still-present Diary. Its initial dimensions
may adapt to the projection family and amount of admitted information, within
bounds that preserve useful Diary context behind it. An in-application window
is preferred to a browser popup because it can preserve focus, accessibility,
responsive behaviour and deterministic history. A future explicit detach
affordance may be considered separately.

Phone layouts may necessarily occupy most of the viewport, but they retain the
same projection contract and deterministic return path.

## Date-first page choreography

When a request targets a different Diary day, the underlying Diary moves
first. The accepted interaction sequence is:

1. interpret and proofread the requested date;
2. navigate the ordinary Diary and complete the authoritative fresh read;
3. show a short direction-aware page-turn or capped riffling transition;
4. verify that the intended date is now current; and
5. open the foreground projection over that Diary page.

The animation never substitutes for loading or verification. Long jumps are
visually capped rather than animating every intervening day. Reduced-motion
users receive an immediate or simple dissolve transition.

This choreography makes the causal relationship legible: Reception One is a
focused view of the Diary, not a second Diary.

## The Bureau

The integrated architecture uses three deliberately separate desks:

- **Agent at the typewriter:** a bounded cognitive work cell interprets one
  admitted natural-language request and emits a typed, non-authoritative
  intent/projection draft. It cannot source arbitrary facts, render the
  product, select identity, assert availability or perform a command.
- **Proofreader/sub-editor:** a deterministic egress gate validates exact
  schema, scope, grounding, freshness, authority and atomic consistency. It
  may make only allowlisted mechanical repairs. Ambiguity becomes a typed
  clarification; an unsafe or ungrounded draft releases nothing.
- **Typesetter:** deterministic product code consumes only proofreader-admitted
  typed fields plus fresh authorised Diary reads. It turns the underlying
  Diary page and composes the appropriate projection. It never displays raw
  provider output or hidden reasoning.

FastAPI/PostgreSQL and the existing API Spine remain the only authority for
identity, availability, conflicts, confirmation, writes, idempotency, audit
and receipts.

## Safety language belongs at the right layer

The authored-synthetic demonstration should begin with one concise
acknowledgement:

> All patients, clinicians and appointments in this demonstration are
> fictional. Nothing you do will affect a real Diary.

After acknowledgement, a quiet `Synthetic demo` marker and an optional details
link are sufficient for provenance orientation. Operational messages such as
`Proposal only - nothing booked`, ambiguity clarification and stale-state
blocks remain visible where they matter. Those messages explain present
product state; they are not legal ceremony.

Research consent, withdrawal and recording information remain obligations of a
future participant protocol. They should be supplied clearly without turning
the product surface itself into a consent form.

## Text before deliberate voice

The first integrated Bureau slice is text-first:

`natural-language request -> typed draft -> deterministic proofreader ->
fresh Diary read -> page turn -> foreground projection -> safe return`

Once the text path demonstrates value, explicit push-to-talk should enter
through the same request contract. A visible, editable transcript precedes
interpretation. Ambient listening, retained audio and production voice remain
separate future decisions.

## Authority not opened by this decision

This direction grants no provider call, model-connected product runtime, voice
capture, real participant session, real or product-derived data, appointment
write, deployment, release or public-brand authority. The earlier Sydney
Vertex and visual-synthesis ledgers remain consumed. Any bounded Vertex text
lane requires a fresh exact authorization.
