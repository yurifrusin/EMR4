the complete existing status family and the complete existing update family

That review should decide whether the exact existing delete/cancel proposal and

## Why this is a real milestone and a real fork

The four-button console has proved that different interaction grammars can
share one truth kernel without making the renderer authoritative. Status is one
complete command family. Practitioner, time and duration are three views of one
complete update-family transaction. A local selection or draft remains inert;
the backend owns current authority, proposal meaning, confirmation, commit,
idempotency, audit and fresh readback.

The remaining visible tasks do not merely add another view onto either accepted
family. Cancellation/delete, check-in/waiting-area and patient self-service each
introduce distinct product meaning, risk and confirmation policy. Standing
authority removes ceremonial pauses for dependency-satisfied descendants; it
does not choose among those material openings.

## Exact repository finding

- Reception One's selected-appointment console contains exactly four actions:
  status, time, duration and practitioner.
- Its bridge exposes `setAppointmentStatus` and the update-family methods, but
  no cancellation/delete entry.
- The typed Reception One planner can already describe a cancellation review,
  but marks it `proposal_only` with
  `operational_command_available: false`. That is presentation reach, not
  command authority.
- The API Spine declares a distinct `proposeAppointmentDelete` and
  `confirmAppointmentDeleteProposal` family.
- The ordinary Diary's `deleteBooking` first requests the delete proposal. On
  a 404 it falls back to the status proposal for `Cancelled`, explicitly
  omitting `cancellation_reason`.
- `applySignedDeleteProposal` then accepts either the allowlisted status-confirm
  or delete-confirm endpoint and chooses the matching idempotency derivation.
- The ordinary path does perform explicit staff interaction and ends with a
  fresh Diary reload, but its dual-family fallback and reason preservation need
  an exact readiness decision before a new renderer reuses it.

The finding does not assert that the fallback is exploitable or incorrect. It
means cancellation has not yet acquired the single-family, one-kernel clarity
that status and update now possess.

## Recommended first cancellation tranche

If Yuri selects cancellation, freeze a provider-free repository-only readiness
review with this maximum scope:

1. inventory the exact ordinary Diary delete proposal, optional status fallback,
   confirm endpoint, warning/block, reason-code, cancellation-reason,
   idempotency and fresh-reload branches;
2. compare that source behavior with the API Spine's distinct delete/cancel
   family and current backend route mounts;
3. classify whether Reception One can reuse one existing interaction unchanged
   or whether a bounded delete-family convergence tranche must precede UI;
4. require explicit destructive confirmation in every admissible cancellation
   path, with no model or channel self-confirmation;
5. preserve cancel/Escape, stale, blocked, failure, response-loss, replay and
   fresh current-truth outcomes;
6. add no product source, route, database, command or event change; and
7. finish by freezing either one bounded cancellation composition plan or an
   exact fail-closed prerequisite.

Only after that readiness result should a later UI tranche consider a fifth
progressive-disclosure control or another equally clear presentation. The
readiness review must not prejudge the console shape.

## Delegated authority and revocation

Yes: a future Siri-like assistant, email integration or messaging client acts
under a separate, narrow and revocable delegation; it never becomes the
patient. The grant should be audience-restricted, action- and practice-scoped,
short-lived, versioned and checked at every proposal or confirmation attempt.
Revoking it invalidates future use, including a pending confirmation that has
not yet committed.

Revocation is not retroactive database editing. If an authorised booking has
already committed, it remains Diary truth. Reversing it requires the separately
authorised cancellation or rescheduling command, with its own current-
authority and source-truth checks, audit and receipt. A channel binding or
message address is neither the delegation nor proof of patient identity.

This principle is already present in the channel-neutral foundation. A live
grant store, token lifecycle, revocation propagation and external client remain
future programme work.
