# Reception One integrated Bureau baseline plan

Date: 2026-07-29

Owner: Yuri / GPT Sol

Status: `accepted_closed`

## 1. Authority and purpose

Yuri authorised beginning the next Reception One product tranche after
accepting the Yuri-only internal walkthrough result as
`promising_needs_revision` and `not_ready` for a design partner.

This descendant implements the provider-free integrated Bureau baseline
grounded in those findings. It also prepares a provider-blocked typed
interpretation lane so that a later occupied model experiment can be measured
against the deterministic baseline. It does not revise any historical
Continuity node or reuse any consumed provider ledger.

The two gates are:

1. a complete provider-free Diary, projection, proofreader and typesetter
   experience; and
2. a zero-call, provider-blocked typed interpretation contract.

An occupied provider call is outside this plan.

## 2. Product contract

Reception One is a focused view of one authoritative Diary, not a second
Diary and not a separate browser product.

The baseline must:

- honour the launcher or URL `reference_date` when selecting the initial
  ordinary Diary day;
- move the underlying Diary to a requested different day before opening the
  projection;
- use a short, direction-aware and visually capped page-turn transition, with
  a reduced-motion alternative;
- render Reception One as a content-sized modeless in-application foreground
  window over a still-useful visible portion of the ordinary Diary on desktop
  and tablet;
- make that compact detail window the default surface while offering one
  reversible horizontal expansion into the wider Bureau console, without
  changing the current projection, request, Diary date, route or authority
  state;
- use a near-full-screen but plainly reversible sheet on narrow phones;
- make ordinary close, `Escape` and `Return to Diary` perform the same
  deterministic action and restore focus to the launcher;
- replace policy-heavy and technical product copy with brief receptionist
  conversation;
- reserve `grid` for internal architecture and remove it from receptionist-
  facing copy;
- retain the accepted fresh-read availability, selection, proposal-only,
  privacy, interruption, keyboard and reconciliation contracts; and
- keep every identity, availability, conflict, confirmation, write,
  idempotency, audit and receipt decision in the existing backend boundary.

The preferred Bureau activity language is:

- `Understanding your request`;
- `Checking the Diary`;
- `Preparing the view`; and
- `I need one detail`.

The product may show a concise `Synthetic demo` marker and operational state
such as `Proposal only - nothing booked`. Audit and governance detail belongs
outside the ordinary receptionist surface.

## 3. Deterministic Bureau roles

The baseline keeps the three desks separate:

- **Typewriter:** the existing deterministic request fixtures and, later, one
  bounded model cell may emit only an untrusted typed intent draft.
- **Proofreader:** deterministic code validates exact schema, allowed scope,
  grounding, freshness, authority and atomic consistency. Safe repair is
  limited to whitespace, canonical enum casing and deterministic ordering.
- **Typesetter:** deterministic browser code consumes only admitted fields plus
  authorised fresh Diary reads, moves the Diary date and composes the
  foreground projection.

No raw provider text, hidden reasoning or rejected draft may reach the
typesetter.

## 4. Visual concept boundary

Before presentation code changes, generate and inspect one complete
authored-synthetic visual concept for the primary desktop state. One responsive
repair concept is permitted only if the first concept leaves a concrete mobile
or hierarchy defect.

The concept may use only fictional people, dates and appointments. It is a
design reference, not product evidence, and creates no model-connected product
runtime or residency claim.

The implementation should establish:

- a calm navy, ink and eucalyptus shell;
- the ordinary Diary as legible spatial context rather than a dimmed backdrop;
- a bounded foreground window whose size follows admitted content;
- a compact reference-aligned appointment sheet by default, with a title-bar
  expansion control that reveals the conversational Bureau rail only when the
  receptionist asks for the wider working modality;
- clearly delineated appointment records with the date in a narrow left
  column, vertically assembled time/practitioner/location detail in the
  centre, and quiet status at the right;
- a warm terracotta primary action that gives the natural-language input a
  clear visual endpoint;
- restrained editorial/typewriter character without character illustration,
  faux paper texture or decorative clutter;
- one obvious close/return path;
- conversational hierarchy with appointment-sheet boundaries rather than
  badges, governance copy or policy panels; and
- motion that explains date movement without delaying the user.

## 5. Provider-blocked typed lane

After the provider-free product gate passes, create repository-local contracts
for:

- one authored-synthetic receptionist utterance;
- one exact typed intent draft;
- one deterministic proofreader result;
- one admitted typesetter instruction; and
- one fail-closed provider policy.

The policy must reject all provider execution by default and record:

- `execution_enabled: false`;
- `provider_call_count: 0`;
- `credentials_requested: false`;
- `network_requested: false`;
- `raw_prompt_persisted: false`;
- `raw_response_persisted: false`; and
- `product_delivery_enabled: false`.

The prepared contract may name the intended future lane for comparison only:
Google Vertex AI, `gemini-2.5-flash`, project `bernie-emr4-dev`, exact Bernie
keyless impersonated ADC, `australia-southeast1`, the Sydney regional endpoint,
authored-synthetic text, isolated work cell, deterministic proofreader,
external audit, no fallback and no command authority. Naming those frozen
coordinates does not authorise a call.

## 6. Acceptance gates

### Gate A - rehydration and revision binding

- Complete the five-source Ariadne rehydration.
- Preserve every unrelated worktree change and protected ref.
- Record this plan as a new descendant of
  `reception-one-yuri-internal-walkthrough-result`.
- Increment Continuity and Compass together without revising historical
  nodes.
- Validate graph, map, revision binding and the rendered Compass report.

### Gate B - date-first ordinary Diary

- The authored-synthetic harness opens on its exact reference date.
- A different requested date updates and verifies the underlying Diary before
  projection opening.
- Direction, cap and reduced-motion behavior are deterministic.
- Loading or animation cannot falsely imply a verified date.

### Gate C - foreground projection

- Desktop and tablet retain useful, legible Diary context around the
  content-sized modeless window.
- Phone retains a clear single-step return path.
- Close button, `Escape` and `Return to Diary` converge and restore launcher
  focus.
- Projection state does not replace browser history or require closing the
  tab.
- Horizontal expansion and collapse preserve the same typed projection and
  remain presentation-only, in-memory and keyboard-operable.

### Gate D - language and Bureau behavior

- No receptionist-facing occurrence of `grid` remains in the Reception One
  surface.
- The four frozen Bureau phrases appear in the applicable transition or
  clarification states.
- Synthetic orientation is concise and operational warnings remain
  contextual.
- Proposal-only state is unmistakable.

### Gate E - inherited contracts

- The accepted combined patient/practitioner/date/time/duration flow reaches
  the same proposal-only boundary.
- Fresh-read availability and committed-reschedule reconciliation remain
  unchanged.
- Keyboard, privacy, interruption and ordinary-Diary fallback pass.
- No API, command, database, appointment-write, event-family or provider
  runtime is added.

### Gate F - provider-blocked preparation

- Typed input, draft, proofreader and typesetter schemas validate.
- Positive authored-synthetic examples pass deterministically.
- Unknown fields, ungrounded values, stale context, authority-shaped output and
  non-allowlisted repair fail closed.
- A repository-only verifier proves zero provider calls, zero credential use,
  zero network use and zero product delivery.

### Gate G - rendered and repository verification

- Inspect desktop, tablet and phone layouts in a real browser.
- Require no relevant console/page error, horizontal overflow or inaccessible
  enabled control.
- Compare the primary implementation against the accepted visual concept using
  a fidelity ledger covering composition, Diary visibility, foreground sizing,
  language, close/return, typography, controls and motion.
- Run focused Reception One and API Spine tests, Continuity/Compass validation,
  JSON/schema validation, JavaScript syntax, Python compilation/static checks,
  `git diff --check` and process/network residue checks.
