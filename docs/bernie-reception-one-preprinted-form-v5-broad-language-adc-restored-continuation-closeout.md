## Transport diagnosis and repair

The first resumed run stopped after five successful provider calls when the
first correction turn could not traverse the one-use relay. Every failed relay
ledger was consumed with zero provider calls.

Bounded diagnostics established the exact defect:

- the typed correction packet was 9,095 bytes;
- the relay accepted at most 8,192 bytes;
- the host broker already accepted at most 32,768 bytes;
- the relay therefore returned HTTP 413 before the broker or Vertex received
  the request; and
- the credential-free cell normalized the non-JSON relay body to
  `invalid_json`.

The relay ceiling now matches the pre-existing 32 KiB broker ceiling. The
readiness check observes the listening socket without consuming the one-use
connection. Pre-connect refusal retries exist on both cell-to-relay and
relay-to-host hops, and no retry occurs after a request can have been sent.
Local failure diagnostics retain bounded codes, byte counts and hashes only.
Focused regressions, provider-blocked execution, real isolation and the exact
ADC/cloud-control preflight passed before the successful recovery.
