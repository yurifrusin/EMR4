## Objective

Consolidate public Australian workflow and clinical sources, synthetic-health
methodology, medical-practice-system documentation and copyright-safe fictional
practice prompts into one traceable scenario-source hierarchy. Freeze the
smallest non-duplicative next architecture tranche that can reuse EMR4's
existing reception semantic specification and stateful replay harness while
supporting later administrative, waiting-room and clinical-diagnostic cases.

Record Yuri's revised data direction precisely:

- real patient or practice data is not admitted directly to development cases;
- wholly authored synthetic scenarios remain the default development material;
- provider-live evaluation over wholly synthetic cases is eligible only under
  a separately frozen execution envelope;
- the timestamped historical diary snapshots may be considered later for a
  local-only privacy feasibility study and aggregate calibration; and
- no raw snapshot, recognisable real day or exact high-frequency sequence may
  enter a fixture, prompt or provider call under this orientation.

## Evidence-source hierarchy

Every source is assigned one closed class before it can influence a scenario:

1. `normative_or_clinical_guidance` may support a requirement, safety
   constraint or clinical oracle within its stated jurisdiction and scope.
2. `accepted_emr4_contract` may support expected product behavior already
   accepted in the repository.
3. `method_or_interoperability_standard` may control scenario design,
   validation, coverage or interchange, but not a clinical conclusion.
4. `vendor_operational_documentation` and `vendor_advertised_capability` may
   reveal workflow vocabulary and candidate edge cases; neither proves safety,
   efficacy, prevalence or the correct outcome.
5. `fiction_prompt_only` may contribute abstract human-factor tensions. It has
   zero clinical, legal, privacy, billing or workflow-oracle authority.
6. `private_observed_calibration` remains closed until a separate local-only
   feasibility and privacy review passes. If admitted later, it may weight
   distributions or motifs but may not become an outcome oracle or a replayable
   day-level fixture.
7. `local_design_assumption` must remain visibly labelled and independently
   reviewable; it may not masquerade as an external fact.

## Scenario architecture decision

The next architecture must be a domain-neutral traceability envelope around,
not a replacement for, domain-specific scenarios.

The envelope will hold:

- stable scenario identity, version, domain and evidence label;
- structured source records with class, locator, jurisdiction, version,
  retrieval date, rights posture, supported claim and limitation;
- wholly fictional actors, roles, authority ceilings and practice context;
- coverage links to requirements, hazards, features and evidence levels;
- an oracle bundle separating deterministic truth, authority/safety rules and
  model-quality rubrics;
- execution bindings to existing domain-specific fixtures and harnesses;
- author, extractor, adjudicator and reviewer separation;
- transformation lineage and counterfactual/metamorphic relationships; and
- an optional opaque `CalibrationEvidenceRef` that cannot resolve private data.

Provider-free deterministic checks remain the acceptance oracle. A provider may
later interpret or realise a surface utterance and may be evaluated repeatedly,
but it cannot modify canonical truth, author its own oracle, certify its own
output or gain command authority.

## Historical diary direction

Yuri reports scores of timestamp-named snapshots per day over several months,
created as the current-day Diary was refreshed for workstation polling. This
is sufficient in principle to derive interval-censored changes by differencing
adjacent states. A later feasibility review may design:

1. a local-only, no-network format and field inventory;
2. a deterministic adjacent-snapshot differencer;
3. typed relative-time events with direct identifiers and free text removed;
4. rare-sequence suppression, coarse bucketing and aggregate transition/motif
   estimation;
5. generation of new synthetic days rather than publication of observed days;
   and
6. linkage, membership and recognisability attacks against the proposed output.

The filename time is an observation boundary, not necessarily the exact event
time. A derived event must preserve `observed_after` and `observed_by` rather
than inventing second-level precision.

No claim of complete or irreversible de-identification is permitted before that
separate review. `synthetic_calibrated_from_private_aggregates` must remain
distinct from `wholly_authored_synthetic`, `deidentified_observed_sequence` and
`raw_private_observation`.
