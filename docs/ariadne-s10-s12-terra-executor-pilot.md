# Ariadne S10-S12 Terra Executor Pilot

Status: revised after S10; continuing through S11-S12

Sol remains at the S10 tranche-entry gate and the final S12 acceptance gate.
GPT 5.6 Terra/high handles routine intra-tranche planning and execution. Terra
may allocate and dispatch configured workers, request same-lane corrections,
run deterministic gates, and integrate accepted commits onto a staging branch.

Terra cannot change Sol's tranche direction, expand scope or authority, or
self-authorize protected `master` integration. Those boundaries prevent economy
from silently changing product direction or executive authority.

The pilot escalates to Sol for scope or authority changes, ambiguous product
policy, repeated worker defects, security/write/deployment/release boundaries,
conflicting evidence, and protected-master integration variance. Luna is recorded as a
future mechanical receipt coordinator but is disabled for this first pilot.

The closeout records Sol interventions, retries, invalid integrations,
escalations, correction commits, and duplicated-context events. The pilot is
successful only if it reduces gate-owner involvement without increasing final
correction work.

## S10 Result

The worker/executor split worked, but mandatory Conductor participation did
not. S10 required two Conductor rejoinders, two Sol authority escalations, and
thousands of lines of coordination evidence before a bounded test-only result
could close. This cost exceeded the expected drift-control benefit.

Sol now selects the planning mode. Terra is the default planner and executor
for routine bounded sprints and may allocate configured workers on a staging
branch. Sol retains tranche direction, escalation decisions, and
protected-master authorization. Terra may execute an exact Sol-approved master
integration manifest, but must stop on any conflict, changed SHA, unexpected
diff, failed required test, scope drift, or security/release implication. A
Conductor is optional and invoked only when Sol wants independent challenge or
the sprint crosses an ambiguity, architecture, security, authority,
repeated-failure, or complex-allocation boundary.

The default economical worker pool is DeepSeek 4 Flash and Gemini 3.5 Flash via
Antigravity. Selection is capability-ranked per task; Gemini is not restricted
to UX. DeepSeek 4 Pro is the first economical Conductor consultant when one is
needed, with Fable and Opus reserved for higher-leverage specialist review.
