## Frozen finding to test

In rc.7, `concludeTurn()` marks an execution. The successful result obtains
`concludesTurn: true` when the registry creates that result. Post-execute runs
after dispatch result creation. Therefore a conclusion request made only after
`await next()` in `tools/post-execute` is too late to alter the already-created
successful result. A safe future runner must request conclusion after the
exact pre-execute boundary accepts the root edit and before dispatch, then
observe the post-policy decision and authoritative final `tools/result`
separately.

This is a source-bound hypothesis until the deterministic evidence passes.

## Closed coordinate model

Every admitted observation has exactly one coordinate:

- `edit_success_accept_concluded`;
- `edit_success_accept_late_marker`;
- `edit_error_accept_not_concluded`;
- `edit_success_blocked_not_concluded`;
- `post_execute_decision_failed_not_concluded`.

The typed record separately carries:

- input result kind: `success` or `error`;
- post-execute decision kind: `accept`, `block` or `failed`;
- conclusion request stage:
  `pre_execute_after_boundary_accept`, `post_execute_after_decision` or
  `not_requested`;
- authoritative final result kind:
  `success_concluding`, `success_nonconcluding` or `error`;
- terminal turn kind: `completed` or `error`.

Unknown keys, unknown values, inconsistent combinations and duplicate
coordinates fail closed.
