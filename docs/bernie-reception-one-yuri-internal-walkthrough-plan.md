## Purpose

Prepare a concise self-facilitated walkthrough that lets Yuri examine the
current Reception One implementation carefully and record formative product
criticism. This is an internal diagnostic, not a representative-participant
session and not a usability-threshold run.

The accepted representative-staff kit remains unchanged as historical
readiness evidence. This descendant supplies a separate internal surface.

The walkthrough may:

- use the existing authored-synthetic Reception One and ordinary Diary;
- present the existing eight Stage 3B task scenarios without counterbalancing;
- begin with one concise synthetic/no-write acknowledgement;
- retain task ratings and optional product-design notes in browser memory;

  fixture with the model provider disabled; and

- claim representative usability, threshold attainment or production fitness;
- call Vertex or another provider;
- capture voice, audio, video, screen recording or ambient input;
- use real patient, practitioner, practice, product-derived, clinical or
  historical material;
- offer or invoke appointment confirmation or another product write;
- use network telemetry, cookies, local storage, session storage, IndexedDB or
  background persistence; or
- deploy, release, commit, push or move protected refs.

Optional notes are local internal product criticism. The interface instructs
Yuri not to enter real names, identifiers, practice details or copied prompt
text. The export states accurately that it contains free-form product notes
when they are supplied.

## Participant-facing correction

The opening gate contains one acknowledgement rather than participant codes,
practice buckets, counterbalance arms and four separate attestations. After the
gate, only a quiet `Internal - synthetic` marker remains.

No threshold is calculated. The walkthrough records:

- whether the task worked;
- whether orientation was clear;
- whether Reception One was more useful than the ordinary Diary;
- whether ordinary Diary fallback was used;
- allowlisted friction flags; and
- an optional bounded product-design note.

The finish section asks whether the current product is ready for a design
partner and records support, neutrality or concern for the foreground-window,
date-turn, Bureau and text-before-voice directions.
