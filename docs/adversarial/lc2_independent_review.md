## 1. Scope and Purpose

This document serves as the official docs record summarizing the independent adversarial review of the DW2 candidate corpus, and details the six adversarial probes generated to stress-test the factory promotion rules and quarantine queue.

Every outer probe record has expected disposition `quarantine`, and its
Gemini-authored candidate keeps `judge_identity` null. Mutated payloads may
deliberately claim forbidden Gold, adjudicated, promoted, certified, or
accepted state in order to test fail-closed handling; those claims are attack
inputs, never corpus status or Gemini certification.

---

## 2. Summary of Adversarial Attacks and Expected Outcomes

We have authored six representative JSON fixtures under `tests/fixtures/bernie_corpus_adversarial/` to cover the six required attack vectors:

### 2.1. Tier Escalation
- **Fixture File:** `tests/fixtures/bernie_corpus_adversarial/adversarial_probes_tier_escalation.json`
- **Attack Description:** A model-generated candidate attempts a masquerade attack by declaring `provenance: "gold"` and `adjudication: "adjudicated"` directly in its wrapper, without any of the required promotion history or adjudication records.
- **Expected Outcome:** `CorpusCandidate` validation rejects the payload because promoted model-generated Gold lacks promotion evidence. An importing harness records this fail-closed validation result as `schema_invalid` quarantine; the promotion engine is not reached.

### 2.2. Self-Certification Bypass
- **Fixture File:** `tests/fixtures/bernie_corpus_adversarial/adversarial_probes_self_certification.json`
- **Attack Description:** A candidate generated by `gemini-3.5-flash` attempts to be promoted using an adjudication record whose judge is also `gemini-3.5-flash`, utilizing different lane/transport labels (`ag-lane-generator` vs `ag-lane-judge`) to disguise identity.
- **Expected Outcome:** The self-certification guard compares canonical model IDs, detects the identity collision, and quarantines the candidate with reason `self_certification`.

### 2.3. Authority Breach
- **Fixture File:** `tests/fixtures/bernie_corpus_adversarial/adversarial_probes_authority_breach.json`
- **Attack Description:** A Silver candidate attempts to sneak in write authority by setting `provider_write: true` and `confirmation: true` in its `authority_grant` block.
- **Expected Outcome:** Normal `CorpusCandidate` validation rejects the non-empty authority grant before promotion. The promotion engine's `generator_authority_grant` check remains defence in depth for unsafely constructed models.

### 2.4. Schema Violation
- **Fixture File:** `tests/fixtures/bernie_corpus_adversarial/adversarial_probes_schema_violation.json`
- **Attack Description:** A candidate has a missing required field (`reference_date`) in its embedded `scenario` block.
- **Expected Outcome:** Deserialization/validation fails and the candidate is quarantined with reason `schema_invalid`.

### 2.5. Semantic Drift
- **Fixture File:** `tests/fixtures/bernie_corpus_adversarial/adversarial_probes_semantic_drift.json`
- **Attack Description:** A candidate claims semantic equivalence to a Gold seed, but the utterance silently changes the time from 3pm to 4pm while the metadata and deltas still claim 3pm (15:00).
- **Expected Outcome:** Machine checks pass validation (since the text is present in the utterance), but the judge/reviewer rejects the candidate during independent review. When the rejected adjudication is submitted, the promotion engine quarantines the candidate with reason `adjudication_rejected`.

### 2.6. Quarantine Bypass
- **Fixture File:** `tests/fixtures/bernie_corpus_adversarial/adversarial_probes_quarantine_bypass.json`
- **Attack Description:** A candidate attempts to bypass the quarantine check and achieve promotion to Gold by providing an adjudication record with an incomplete scope (e.g. `checked_semantic_scope` contains only `["action"]`).
- **Expected Outcome:** Incomplete scopes fail the AdjudicationRecord Pydantic validators, leading to quarantine with reason `schema_invalid`.
