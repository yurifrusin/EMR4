# EMR4 Rayleen, Davida and controlled-recovery development plan

Date: 2026-08-04

Status: Gate -1 architecture review in progress; all Gate-zero and lane dispatch
remains paused

Architecture parent:
`docs/emr4-model-required-deterministic-authority-bureau-architecture.md`

## Objective

Prepare the next development sequence without opening it. The sequence makes a
provider model mandatory in every named intelligent loop while keeping typed
proofreading, deterministic authority, human gates, commands and post-action
verification independent and mandatory.

The current fifth Bernie/Davida pair remains the completed candidate parent.
This plan neither starts its material live-backend/Davida-write successor nor
dispatches any worker.

## Programme gate -1: adversarial containment review

Before Gate zero, treat every provider model and cognitive wrapper as capable of
following direct or indirect prompt injection and of emitting deliberately
hostile output. Gate -1 is architecture and evidence only; it does not run a
provider, wire a model runtime, open product data or add an actuator.

The evidence-backed Gate -1 candidate selects two required properties:

- one shared deterministic label/capability envelope that propagates
  provenance, integrity, confidentiality/readers, freshness and authority
  ceilings across context, candidate fields, transformations, command arguments
  and response/egress sinks; and
- one fresh, broker-mediated, deny-by-default cognitive cell per Bureau attempt,
  with exactly one typed input and one typed candidate output and no ambient
  shell, code, tool, path, URL, callback, filesystem, database, credential,
  metadata, network or actuator bridge.

Provider-model participation remains mandatory for the intelligent dialogue.
The model and cell remain untrusted candidate generators. Model safeguards,
prompt-injection detection and adversarial evaluation are defense in depth and
cannot supply authority.

Gate -1 passes only after the hardening portfolio, attack paths, comparable
alternatives, diagrams, threat-model delta and deterministic artifact checks
pass a fresh independent Gemini 3.6 Flash/high veto. Until then the selections
are candidates and Gate zero remains closed.

## Programme gate zero: shared architecture contract

Before the three lanes diverge, freeze one versioned contract covering:

- the four-plane Bureau structure;
- mandatory provider-model admission and explicit outage semantics;
- Bernie, Rayleen, Davida and recovery-domain ownership;
- shared typed-candidate and deterministic-proofreader envelope conventions;
- the Gate -1 label/capability algebra, trusted source wrappers, complete sink
  mediation, endorsement/declassification rules and typed denial receipts;
- context-frame provenance, freshness, minimization and authorization;
- the Gate -1 one-attempt cell, provider broker, hostile-byte parser, bridge
  registry, resource quotas, teardown and residue contract;
- separate cognitive, authority and actuator principals;
- human and dual-review policy expression;
- no silent provider fallback; and
- exact evidence labels for provider-free component tests versus occupied model
  and live product paths.

Proposed artifacts are architecture, schema prototypes, a threat-model delta
and deterministic artifact tests only. No provider call or runtime wiring is
part of gate zero.

Gate-zero acceptance requires Sol Extra High architectural review and one fresh
Gemini 3.6 Flash/high independent veto after all deterministic checks pass.

## Lane A: Rayleen waiting-room intelligence

### A1. Read and projection boundary

Design a minimal, practice-scoped, freshness-bound
`WaitingRoomContextFrame`. It should expose only the operational fields needed
for the authorized surface and should distinguish backend fact from derived
display signal.

Define deterministic projections for:

- current arrivals and waiting states;
- waiting-area and practitioner grouping;
- elapsed wait and threshold bands;
- longest-wait and flow exceptions;
- selected patient/practitioner/location focus; and
- uncluttered intent-projected waiting-room views.

No model, route mounting, patient client or write occurs in A1.

### A2. Rayleen NLU and dialogue evaluation foundation

Define a Rayleen-specific intent grammar and authored-synthetic language corpus
for read, explain, filter, group, focus, clarify, check-in proposal, status
proposal and waiting-area-move proposal. Reuse the shared Diary action grammar
and confirmation semantics rather than creating Rayleen-private actions.

The evaluator must separate:

- linguistic interpretation;
- deterministic projection correctness;
- identity ambiguity;
- stale or conflicting state;
- proposal versus confirmation; and
- safe refusal.

Provider-free candidate fixtures test the proof plane, but A2 cannot claim the
Rayleen intelligent interaction works end to end.

### A3. Occupied model-required Rayleen rehearsal

Only after separate provider, data-class, region, identity, cost and call
authority, connect one approved provider model through Access AI. The model
receives typed authored-synthetic context and emits a closed candidate. The
proofreader either admits it, requests one bounded correction or rejects it.

No patient data or product write is opened by A3.

### A4. Product read and UI projection

After a fresh product-read decision, connect authorized waiting-room truth to
Rayleen's deterministic projection engine and present the admitted model-
selected view in Reception One. This requires privacy, role, interruption,
accessibility, stale-response and cross-practice evidence.

### A5. Arrival/status/waiting-area command descendants

Each write is a separate material gate. Reuse or deliberately extend the
existing status proposal/confirm path. Check-in and waiting-area movement must
prove signed or server-held confirmation evidence, current identity and scope,
idempotency, atomic status/waiting-area/audit/outbox behavior, replay rejection
and deterministic readback. Rayleen cannot confirm her own proposal.

Patient-facing Rayleen remains later still and additionally requires external
identity proofing, anti-enumeration, rate limits, privacy assessment and patient
experience review.

## Lane B: Davida NLU parity and institutional intelligence

### B1. Practice-administration intent grammar

Create a dedicated grammar for read, explain, summarize, compare, validate,
dry-run, propose, clarify and refuse. Initial resources remain active
practitioners and locations, then expand only through separately reviewed
context frames.

The grammar must not reuse Bernie's patient or appointment assumptions and must
never convert `propose` into `confirm` or `administer`.

### B2. Authored-synthetic corpus and evaluator

Build ordinary-development language cases for incomplete names, cross-location
requests, inactive resources, stale context, apparently harmless but
security-relevant changes, bulk wording, negation, correction and attempts to
delegate confirmation to Davida.

Measure model interpretation separately from deterministic grounding and
policy. Protected holdouts, real practice data and external corpora remain
closed.

### B3. Occupied model-required advisory rehearsal

Under fresh provider authority, the model conducts dialogue and emits one
closed practice-administration candidate. The existing deterministic context
desk, advisory proofreader and dry-run boundary become the proof plane. No
administrative write is included.

### B4. Administrative command implementations

Implement command families one at a time, beginning only after a material write
decision. The already designed practitioner default-location confirmation is a
candidate first command. Davida remains provenance-bearing proposer only; a
freshly authorized human is the confirmer and deterministic readback proves the
result.

## Lane C: controlled recovery intelligence

### C1. System-anatomy and observation contract

Define a version-bound, read-only technical graph containing service versions,
deployment manifest references, schema head, dependency state, health and
capacity signals, sanitized error classes, backup verification, configuration
drift and the signed runbook catalog.

The graph must exclude credentials, unrestricted logs, raw patient/clinical
data and generic database introspection. Deterministic collectors label every
observation with source, time, freshness and confidence class.

### C2. Diagnostic candidate and proofreader

Define strict model output schemas for hypotheses, evidence links, missing
evidence, likely impact, urgency, candidate runbook and user explanation.
Require an approved provider model for the diagnostic dialogue. The proofreader
must reject unsupported causes, absent evidence, stale anatomy versions,
unknown runbooks and any free-form executable instruction.

C2 is read-only and has no actuator.

### C3. Recovery-plan and authority policy

Define deterministic risk tiers and authority requirements. At minimum:

- observe/explain only;
- reversible scoped service recovery;
- human-approved rollback or failover;
- dual-reviewed database, security or data-supply operation; and
- forbidden autonomous actions.

Each plan names exact preconditions, environment, target, expected effect,
maximum blast radius, rollback, expiry, idempotency, audit and postconditions.

### C4. Allowlisted actuator rehearsal

Only after a material actuator decision, build a provider-free authored-
synthetic simulator that accepts one-use typed execution evidence for exact
runbooks. It must reject shell/SQL text from the model, unknown parameters,
scope expansion, stale plans, replay and partial verification.

No production deployment or real database is part of C4.

### C5. Live development recovery

Any live development action requires separate exact target and authority. Begin
with a disposable, non-PHI, reversible fault in an isolated environment. The
provider model remains mandatory for diagnosis and explanation; deterministic
policy and human approval remain mandatory for execution where the risk tier
requires them.

## Lane D: application, database and reference-update supply chain

### D1. Separate update classes

Freeze distinct contracts for:

- application builds and dependency updates;
- database schema migrations;
- pharmaceutical, vaccination and other reference datasets; and
- operational or clinical policy content.

They must not share an undifferentiated generic update command.

### D2. Signed provenance and semantic-delta proof

Every reference or policy update needs source identity, licence, checksum,
schema version, jurisdiction, issued/effective/expiry dates, supersession and
withdrawal metadata. Deterministic validation produces a typed semantic delta,
compatibility decision and rollback candidate before the provider model
explains the update or forms an activation proposal.

### D3. Staged promotion and rollback

Plan shadow import, validation, canary scope, human or dual review, atomic
activation, immutable audit, post-activation readback and last-known-good
rollback. A provider model participates in the operator dialogue and plan, but
cannot certify source authenticity, approve itself or activate the update.

Actual download, licence acceptance, database import, migration, activation or
production change is a separate material gate.

## Parallelization after gate zero

After explicit instruction to begin, A1/A2, B1/B2 and C1/C2 are separable
architecture/test lanes and can proceed concurrently. D1/D2 may begin alongside
C1 after the shared technical context and provenance vocabulary is frozen.

The integration path remains single-track:

1. Sol owns architecture, acceptance and integration.
2. DeepSeek V4 Flash/high may implement bounded mechanical artifacts.
3. Gemini 3.6 Flash/high performs fresh independent veto review after
   deterministic gates.
4. No worker accepts its own output or moves protected refs.

No worker packet is authorized or issued by this plan.

- transmitting authored-synthetic, product-derived, identity, patient,
  clinical, operational or technical data externally;

- running a database migration or backup restore;

Gate -1 additionally forbids treating a detector, delimiter, model refusal,
human confirmation or sandbox alone as proof that prompt injection cannot
affect authority. Any proposed new cognitive-cell bridge is a fresh security
architecture decision.
