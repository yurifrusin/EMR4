## Terminal and correction rule

Inside the one session, DeepSeek may naturally read, attempt an edit, receive a
tool error, reread and correct its edit. This is ordinary agentic work, not an
orchestrator retry.

After a terminal failure:

1. if the model never received the work packet and the cause is one directly
   attributable mechanical envelope defect, Sol may correct it without new
   diagnostic infrastructure and perform at most one fresh monitored run;
2. otherwise, do not rerun the same Harness task, add a provider-free
   diagnostic tranche or silently fall back;
3. preserve the trace, recover the development task under Sol or another
   explicitly allocated transport, and try the native Harness later on a
   different bounded assignment.

A correction that needs a new runner, broker, guard graph, package archaeology
or synthetic reproduction series is not “one mechanical correction.” It ends
the native lane for that assignment.
