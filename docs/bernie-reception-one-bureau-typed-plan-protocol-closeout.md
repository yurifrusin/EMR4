Date: 2026-07-29

Result: `reception_one_bureau_typed_plan_protocol_provider_free_pass`

## Outcome

The provider-free Reception One Bureau Typed Plan Protocol passes with
authored-synthetic evidence.

Reception One now has a provider-neutral language between a future
sandboxed interpreter and the deterministic proofreader. A candidate planner
may compose only fourteen named operators. Those operators resolve already
supplied synthetic references, perform authorised in-memory reads, search or
assess candidate slots, and prepare proposal-only outputs. They cannot
confirm, book, move, resize, cancel or change appointment status.

The protocol proves two complementary paths:

1. known create, move, resize, cancel and status requests reuse the accepted
   deterministic semantic extractor and map to existing API Spine proposal
   operation identifiers; and
2. a novel squeeze-in request is represented as a safe composition of
   schedule read plus squeeze-in assessment, producing candidate information
   and warnings for human review without inventing a new primitive or write.

- Independent plan-hash and context-revision recheck at execution.
- Provider-free, network-free, database-free and authored-synthetic evidence.

## What this proves

This proves that a model-independent, typed dialogue can safely express both
known Diary proposal families and one useful novel composition, while a
deterministic proofreader retains grounding, freshness, type, effect and
authority control.

novel receptionist language, safe product-data access, provider residency,
runtime database behavior, representative usability, appointment mutation,
voice interaction, production fitness, deployment or release readiness.
