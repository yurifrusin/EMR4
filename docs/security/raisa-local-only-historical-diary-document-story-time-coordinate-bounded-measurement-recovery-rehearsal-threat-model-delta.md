## Threats and controls

### A recovery becomes a retry of consumed private evidence

Threat: the earlier `v1` attempt is reused, resumed or tuned after its timeout.

Control: only a new literal `v2` root is admissible. The old attempt is never
read by the runner. The new root must be absent before its single metadata bind,
and its content terminal permits exactly one run with no retry.

### A longer timeout merely strands Word for longer

Threat: the 1,800-second ceiling increases process risk without containment.

Control: accepted authored-synthetic evidence first proved typed timeout and
exact parent cleanup. The parent may stop only a PID whose `WINWORD` class and
UTC start-time ticks match. Missing or mismatched identity blocks; process-name
fallback is forbidden. The pre-existing process set must be preserved.

### Count-only progress becomes a side channel

Threat: progress reveals individual document timing or private layout values.

Control: progress admits aggregate counts plus coarse elapsed/rate buckets
only. It has no filename, path, document identifier, exact timestamp, source
text, time value, page, coordinate, distance, key or mapping. Strict extra-
forbid validation and cleanup apply.

### Main-story parsing broadens private extraction

Threat: names, notes or contact values outside tables cross the process pipe.

Control: PowerShell parses complete time tokens locally and emits only integer
minutes with private ephemeral coordinates. Main-story raw text never enters
Python, retained files, stdout, exceptions, Git, models or providers.

### Coordinate proximity invents scheduling time

Threat: a distant, cross-page, ambiguous or unavailable label is selected.

Control: the mapper is frozen before access: same adjusted page, at most 16
quarter-points, unique nearest minute, different-time ties unmapped and no
fallback from layout, metadata or expected clinic rhythm.

### Private coordinates survive reduction

Threat: page/vertical values or mappings become a reversible layout record.

Control: coordinates are private inputs only and do not enter the private
projection or public aggregate. Cleanup removes manifest, projection, keys,
mappings, coordinates, control and progress files for every terminal result.

### A useful aggregate silently opens first use

Threat: a passing local measurement is immediately turned into a reusable
scenario or product benchmark.

Control: the first-use gate remains explicitly default-deny. This tranche may
retain only a non-PHI aggregate ignored-local reading. Any particular reusable
derivative requires a later candidate-specific plan and admission result.

### Re-identification risk is overstated or understated

Threat: a structural aggregate is called anonymous, or useful trajectory
structure is dismissed as harmless without examining a candidate.

Control: the result makes no anonymity claim and grants no downstream use.
Rare structural trajectories remain private. The later first-use gate, if
reached, evaluates a concrete minimized derivative rather than a blanket claim
about the trove.

## Residual risk

Historical `.doc` layout performance may differ materially from synthetic
`.docx` throughput. Word pagination can depend on local rendering state.
Rendered alignment shows structural proximity to a time label but does not
prove that a segment is a patient booking rather than a break, roster note or
composite entry. A truthful contained low-utility result therefore remains an
acceptable terminal and cannot be retried in this tranche.
