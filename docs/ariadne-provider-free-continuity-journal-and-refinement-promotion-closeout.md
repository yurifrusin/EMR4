## Outcome

Three useful Prime Agent ideas have been translated into a narrow Ariadne
development-harness sidecar without installing Prime Agent or borrowing its
runtime:

1. an append-only, generation-aware operation journal that can distinguish a
   new submission, an exact completed replay, a conflicting request, live work
   and work that requires explicit recovery;
2. deterministic reuse of an unchanged gate result, while preserving an exact
   failure for diagnosis and uncertainty for resolution; and
3. quarantined refinement proposals that require explicit validation,
   separated promotion authority and immutable rollback history.

The implementation is deliberately decision-only. It does not append durable
state, execute or replay a command, alter a policy, prompt, skill or source
file, spawn a process, contact a provider, or touch Raisa runtime state.
