## Objective

Measure the unchanged, untaught v5 form across twenty-four newly authored
synthetic utterance cases. The cohort broadens language shape without changing
the pre-printed form, prompt, typed language, proofreader or provider binding.

The accepted six-case result remains immutable and is not replayed.

## Frozen cohort

The cases comprise:

- four create variants, including alias, receptionist preface and correction;
- four move variants, including synonym, minimal wording and correction;
- four resize variants;
- three cancellation variants;
- three status variants, including one deliberate `no-show` parser gap;
- two squeeze-in variants; and
- four action-tempting ambiguity traps that must remain clarification-only.

The cohort contains single-turn paraphrases, two-turn composition, corrections,
minimal pairs and utterances whose surface detail may tempt an unsupported
action inference.

The following remain byte-for-byte unchanged:

- the v5 system instruction and provider response schema;
- the model-authored fields `operator_note`, `goal_code` and `steps`;
- the broker-owned `version_code: 3`;
- the goal, binding and operator catalogues;
- the compiler, proofreader, executor and operator-note gate;
- temperature zero and thinking budget zero; and
- the no-example, no-demonstration, no-prompt-search condition.

## Oracle and interpretation boundary

Each case is frozen before provider use with:

- the full synthetic utterance sequence;
- the current deterministic semantic extraction;
- expected typed goal and operator sequence;
- expected proposal family or clarification-only disposition; and
- an exact provider-free proofreader-admitted reference output.

The proofreader remains the release authority. The oracle is not placed in the
model prompt and cannot select or repair a provider answer.

The `no-show` case deliberately records a current deterministic-language gap:
the utterance is human-readable as a status request, but the frozen extractor
does not admit that hyphenated form as `status_change`. Its only accepted
outcome in this cohort is clarification. This prevents retrospective scoring
changes and distinguishes proofreader-language coverage from model
form-filling quality.

## Calls and cost

Each case permits one primary call and at most one complete replacement after
an eligible closed proofreader ticket. The absolute ceiling is forty-eight
calls and USD 1. Each case stops at its first admitted or terminal safe result.
There is no third turn, fallback, mid-cohort prompt repair or semantic repair.

## Acceptance and diagnosis

The cohort passes only if every case reaches its frozen safe typed outcome
within budget, no clarification case releases a proposal, every ledger and
audit chain closes, and isolation and cleanup pass.

The diagnostic rule is:

- retain the untaught prompt if every primary reaches its safe outcome;
- nominate the smallest controlled prompt-context comparison only if the same
  correctable model-form/proofreader error recurs in at least three cases;
- nominate deterministic extractor or proofreader-language work, not prompt
  teaching, when failures cluster on a predeclared parser-safe clarification
  gap;
- diagnose isolated or mixed errors without teaching; and
- classify provider/request-contract errors as protocol evidence, not model
  training evidence.
