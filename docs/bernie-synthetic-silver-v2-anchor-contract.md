# Bernie Synthetic Silver V2 Coherent Anchor Contract

Date: 2026-07-17

Status: `frozen_pre_content_candidate`

Authority: Yuri's 2026-07-17 standing synthetic-development authorization

## Objective

Create a genuinely fresh, internally coherent receptionist-to-Bernie Silver
corpus. V2 replaces the current coverage shape rather than relabelling or
rewriting the 102 quarantined v1 rows. It restores balanced clarification and
true whole-action reversal evidence before any further parser refinement.

V2 is ordinary development evidence. It is not Gold, a protected holdout,
certification, real-world receptionist evidence, clinical validation, or
authority to activate a provider, runtime, confirmation, API, database,
deployment, release, or diary write.

## Population and balance

The fresh population contains exactly 96 dialogue-free anchors and two noisy
dialogue candidates per anchor, for 192 candidates.

- six actions: create, move, resize, cancel, status change, and schedule
  explanation;
- eight dialogue forms: one-shot, clarification, correction, reversal,
  ellipsis, anaphora, repeated request, and session restart;
- exactly two anchors for every action/form pair;
- exactly 16 anchors and 32 candidates per action;
- exactly 12 anchors and 24 candidates per dialogue form; and
- one medium- and one high-noise candidate per anchor.

The two anchors in each action/form cell vary bounded temporal or entity
surface conditions. They do not create a second policy meaning for the same
surfaced request.

## Anchor schema and provenance

Each anchor has a new `emr4.bernie.synthetic_noise_seed.v2` identity and binds
one named ordinary-development source scenario only as a provenance base for
fictional entities, reference date, diary state, and action-specific delta
shape. V2 independently freezes its surfaced semantics, clarification state,
expected tools, outcome, appointment deltas, and audit deltas. The source
scenario's dialogue and inconsistent oracle fields are not copied into the
anchor.

Every anchor records:

- the source scenario ID and hash;
- its complete semantic/policy/replay contract;
- a dialogue-form contract describing what must be surfaced;
- required generated-evidence keys;
- a canonical seed hash;
- `contains_source_utterances=false`;
- `protected_holdout_access=false`; and
- every authority grant as false.

Only ordinary LC development evidence may supply base data. Protected V1-V10,
historical diary material, the provenance-blocked appointment-call corpus, and
other external dialogue corpora remain inaccessible.

## Coherent policy and replay invariants

All anchors must pass a deterministic pre-generation coherence validator.

1. A successful mutation has the action-appropriate tool sequence, non-null
   action-appropriate outcome, and exactly the matching appointment and audit
   deltas.
2. A read-only schedule explanation has its read tool sequence,
   `schedule_explained`, and no mutation or audit delta.
3. A clarification anchor has an explicitly ambiguous patient or practitioner,
   a non-null clarification question, at least two bounded choices,
   `request_clarification` as its sole tool, `clarification_required` as its
   outcome, and no appointment or audit delta.
4. A whole-action reversal retains the initially surfaced intended action for
   extraction evaluation but explicitly withdraws that request in its final
   receptionist turn. Its final outcome is null, its appointment and audit
   deltas are empty, and its tool expectation is read-only patient lookup when
   a patient is surfaced or empty when none is needed. It never executes the
   withdrawn action.
5. A correction explicitly names the replaced field and its replacement. The
   final semantic state, tools, outcome, and deltas describe only the corrected
   request.
6. Ellipsis and anaphora recover required values only from earlier turns in the
   same candidate.
7. A repeated request repeats the same request without adding a second action
   or second delta.
8. A session restart explicitly abandons the earlier incomplete request and
   supplies a complete new request whose final semantics alone determine the
   oracle.
9. No expected value may be hidden in an anchor and supplied only to the
   interpreter, replay, or scorer.
10. The current product parser is not an input to anchor coherence or admission.

## Generated dialogue requirements

Each candidate contains receptionist turns only and must surface every value
needed to distinguish its action, final entity state, temporal relation,
normalized values, correction, ambiguity, reversal, or restart. Medium noise
uses at least two allowlisted operations; high noise uses at least three.

Noise may include bounded shorthand, fillers, punctuation/case variation,
speech disfluency, reordered slots, transcript-like fragmentation, local
ellipsis/anaphora, explicit correction/reversal, temporal surface variants,
and harmless distractors. Noise must not change semantics, introduce clinical
facts or identifiers, create a second action, or weaken confirmation and
backend authority.

Every evidence span must exactly slice the generated utterance. Candidate IDs,
seed hashes, generator identity, Silver/pending status, and closed authority
are mechanically validated before semantic review.

## Standing successive-refinement authority

Yuri authorizes the Conductor to progress automatically from v2 admission
through successive evidence-backed synthetic refinements, following the
recommended next step recorded at each accepted stage. This authority covers
ordinary-development corpus engineering, deterministic evaluation, bounded

documentation, protected-PR integration, and baton updates.

The sequence completes when the coherent admitted v2 population passes the
frozen deterministic product contract twice per candidate with perfect safety,
zero variance, exact evidence bindings, active preservation, and independent
acceptance, or when the programme genuinely stalls. It pauses before:

- a material clarification-policy or product-behaviour choice;
- changing replay, scorer, certification, provider/runtime, API/database/UI,
  confirmation, deployment, release, or write authority;
- protected-holdout access or a new certification holdout;
- historical or external corpus access; or
- a material cost, licence, external-coordination, or other user boundary.

No stage may claim that synthetic completion establishes real-world frequency,
clinical safety, production readiness, or whole-product certification.

## Acceptance states

- `v2_admission_pass`: 192/192 fresh candidates are coherent and admitted,
  bindings and balance are exact, safety is closed, and independent review
  passes.
- `v2_partial_pass_with_quarantine`: every admitted candidate is coherent,
  unresolved candidates are explicitly quarantined, balance loss is recorded,
  safety is closed, and independent review passes.
- `revision_required`: contract, provenance, completeness, coherence, binding,
  safety, variance, preservation, or independent evidence fails.

Admission is separate from product robustness. A valid v2 corpus may expose
product gaps; those gaps begin the authorized refinement sequence rather than
invalidating coherent evidence.
