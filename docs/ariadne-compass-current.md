# Ariadne Compass — EMR4

> EMR4 is currently deepening Phase 2B through Reception One: the latest accepted proof shows that one combined receptionist request can remain safely oriented when committed Diary truth changes. This is a working local capability foundation, not a finished Reception One experience or a production-ready EMR.

## North star

**A complete AI-native Australian general-practice management system**

EMR4 joins a Word-centred clinical workspace and a native browser Diary to a FastAPI/PostgreSQL source of truth. Humans retain judgment and confirmation while bounded agents reduce mechanical work without becoming clinical or scheduling authority.

## Programme position

**Reception One scheduling intelligence** — Phase 2B — Bernie Receptionist Copilot

Reception One is the current internal receptionist-copilot programme. Its purpose is to let staff express scheduling intent, see the relevant projection of one authoritative Diary, prepare safe proposals and understand committed changes while the backend retains identity, availability, conflict, write and audit authority.

## Journey so far

1. **Authority foundation — Stage 1 combined appointment-intent foundation**
   Established the supervised patient, practitioner, time and duration proposal/confirmation path while keeping booking truth and writes in the backend. This is the journey foundation.
2. **Interaction grammar — Provider-neutral meta-grid concept**
   Defined one Diary that can be projected into focused, reversible views instead of forcing every task through a full grid. Lineage parent: `stage1-combined-intent-foundation`.
3. **Functional client proof — Functional meta-grid client**
   Made the projection grammar interactive across desktop, tablet, smartphone and keyboard paths without opening a booking write. Lineage parent: `meta-grid-concept`.
4. **Real local plumbing proof — Meta-grid live-local integration evaluation**
   Connected the visible client to real local FastAPI/PostgreSQL reads and proposal boundaries using authored-synthetic data and no provider. Lineage parent: `functional-meta-grid-client`.
5. **Whole-request continuity proof — Reception One combined-scope product proof**
   Proved patient, practitioner, half-hour duration and after-2 constraints together in one request, repairing the continuity gap found by Ariadne without carrying identity into the Compass. Lineage parent: `functional-meta-grid-client`.
6. **Nervous-system foundation — Reception One committed appointment-reschedule event vertical**
   Proved a bounded committed appointment-reschedule signal, authenticated fresh reads and a quiet Reception One cue without making the event a command. Lineage parent: `reception-one-combined-scope-proof`.
7. **Context-preserving change reconciliation — Reception One committed-reschedule availability reconciliation**
   Proved that a committed reschedule can refresh the active availability projection, preserve a still-valid proposal and clear an invalid one without widening write authority. Lineage parent: `reception-one-committed-event-vertical`.

## Current position

**First joined proof of projected intent and committed-change attention — Reception One committed-reschedule availability reconciliation**

**Why this proof came next:** The combined request and the committed-reschedule signal had each been proved separately. The next necessary question was whether Reception One could preserve or withdraw an in-progress availability/proposal context when authoritative Diary truth changed.

A fresh backend slot search now reconciles the active one-practitioner projection after the accepted reschedule event. Valid state survives with fresh candidate data; invalid state and stale navigation history clear; irrelevant or consequence-free changes remain quiet.

### What this unlocks

- A grounded visual/interaction synthesis using a real end-to-end Reception One behaviour rather than a static concept alone.
- A separately authorised decision about whether another typed Diary event family would add enough receptionist value to justify widening the bounded nervous system.
- A concrete programme position from which the Compass can distinguish product advancement from tooling or exploratory work.

### What it does not solve

- The final Reception One visual language, information density or production interaction design.
- Representative receptionist usability, Stage 3B, voice or real-world operational evidence.
- Other Diary event families, general autonomous workflow, provider selection, PII handling, deployment or release.
- The clinical, billing, results, prescribing, patient-portal and other wider EMR4 programme phases.

## Product decision horizon

### Reception One visual and interaction synthesis — candidate

How should the proven conversational, projected and event-aware behaviour be presented as a coherent, uncluttered Reception One experience?

The plumbing now supports a realistic interaction, but the final visual hierarchy and balance between conversation, focused projection and full-Diary fallback remain intentionally unresolved.

Prerequisites:

- A fresh Yuri decision on scope, participants/models and whether the work is exploratory design or implementation.
- Preservation of the accepted projection, proposal, privacy, keyboard and ordinary-Diary fallback contracts.

### Another typed Diary event family — candidate

Is there another committed Diary change whose receptionist value and attention contract justify extending the bounded nervous system?

The reschedule vertical proves the pattern, but breadth should follow an explicit value and threat review rather than event coverage for its own sake.

Prerequisites:

- A fresh Yuri authority decision naming the exact event family and product value.
- A bounded event, fresh-read, relevance, privacy, deduplication and evidence plan.

## Programme-support horizon

### Ariadne workflow-executive maturation — candidate

After the Compass proves useful, should Ariadne compile typed plans and execute bounded worktree, test, review and PR steps through a capability broker?

Routine orchestration could move to deterministic code and modest models, preserving Sol and Yuri attention for programme and product judgment.

Prerequisites:

- Evidence that the Compass improves orientation and that a dry-run plan compiler reduces coordination cost without hiding decisions.
- A separate authority design for capabilities, retries, audit, failure escalation and protected integration.

### Ariadne Synaptic Event Router runtime adapter — candidate

Should the accepted non-executing Synaptic Event Router protocol connect to only the existing default-off local appointment-reschedule feed?

The protocol now proves deterministic scope intersection, mailbox steering, replay suppression and supersession. A real adapter would be the smallest runtime test, but it would newly open authentication, persistence, delivery, failure and retention risks.

Prerequisites:

- The accepted repository-local protocol proof remains the frozen source for scope leases, bilateral routing, fresh-read reconciliation, replay and supersession.
- A fresh Yuri decision naming the exact existing local feed adapter and a runtime threat model before any connection or persistent mailbox.
- Explicit authentication, durable deduplication, persistence, failure, dead-letter and retention decisions while preserving separation from the development Continuity graph.

### Ariadne disposable real-isolation rehearsal — candidate

Should the accepted finite work-cell tape be rehearsed inside one disposable network-disabled, read-only, no-secret local container with no model or product connection?

The in-memory rehearsal now proves deterministic control-plane transitions. A real-isolation rehearsal could next test whether declared container policy matches effective isolation before any adaptive reasoner is considered.

Prerequisites:

- The accepted scripted in-memory rehearsal and Bounded Cognitive Work Cell protocol remain the frozen sources for the tape, proofreader and authority grammar.
- A fresh Yuri decision opening only a disposable real local container plus the exact commands required to build, inspect, run and remove it.
- A threat plan for image provenance, network disablement, read-only inputs, no secrets, resource limits, evidence capture and cleanup, with models, databases, event feeds, product APIs, live mailboxes and commands inside the work cell still closed.

## Decisions that remain Yuri's

- **Should the next Reception One tranche pursue visual/interaction synthesis, another exact event family, or pause product expansion?**
  Required before: Any new Reception One implementation or external design-model dispatch.
- **Should Ariadne progress from a read-only Compass to a dry-run Plan Compiler and later a capability-brokered executor?**
  Required before: Any automatic hook, LLM Conductor call, worker dispatch, worktree action, PR action or protected-ref operation by Ariadne software.
- **Should the accepted Synaptic Event Router protocol connect to the existing default-off local appointment-reschedule feed?**
  Required before: Any operational-DAG scope registry, database/event-feed connection, persistent node mailbox, listener, broker, retry worker or event-driven reconciliation beyond the existing bounded Reception One exception.
- **Should Ariadne run the accepted finite tape in a disposable network-disabled, read-only local container to test real isolation policy?**
  Required before: Any container image build/pull, container command, runtime namespace, mounted rehearsal input or effective-isolation evidence run.

## Map limits

- The canonical continuity graph currently maps the active Reception One lineage and Ariadne tooling, not every EMR4 phase or historical sprint.
- The master implementation plan is a strategic blueprint; phase labels do not prove implementation or readiness.
- Accepted Reception One evidence is local and authored-synthetic unless its source says otherwise; it is not production, release or representative-staff evidence.
- The accepted Synaptic Event Router proof is repository-local authored-synthetic protocol evidence only; a future per-practice operational DAG must remain separate from the development Continuity graph.
- The accepted Bounded Cognitive Work Cell proof describes an unoccupied agent-eligible node and deterministic egress gate; it is not evidence of model, container or product-runtime behaviour.
- The accepted scripted work-cell rehearsal executes only a finite authored tape in process memory; it is not evidence of adaptive cognition, real isolation, persistence, live delivery or product-runtime behaviour.
- The decision horizon records candidates and gates. It neither recommends a winner nor grants authority.
