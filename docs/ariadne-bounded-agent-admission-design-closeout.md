# Ariadne Bounded Agent-Admission Design - Closeout

Date: 2026-07-23

Result: `ariadne_bounded_agent_admission_design_pass`

Owner: GPT Sol High

## Outcome

The bounded agent-admission design passes as a provider-neutral,
repository-local, non-executing protocol. It defines the empty cognition-
adapter slot between a minimal typed context package and the accepted
deterministic proofreader without selecting or invoking a model, provider,
transport or container.

Generated cognition remains a future capability. Nothing in this result can
start it or authorize its output.

## Accepted design

- The admission envelope binds work cell, practice, principal, purpose,
  correlation, policy revision, implementation generation and context digest.
- Six authored-synthetic frames are type-allowlisted, source-labelled,
  purpose-bound, freshness-bound and individually byte/hash bound.
- The instruction plane is independently hashed. User request and fact frames
  are evidence-plane data and cannot alter ports, budgets, capabilities,
  transport, authority or proofreader routing.
- The canonical context occupies 660 canonical payload bytes under an eight-
  frame and 4,096-byte input ceiling.
- Output is limited to the exact five accepted work-cell draft ports under a
  five-draft and 8,192-byte ceiling, with at most two immutable attempts per
  context revision.
- Token accounting remains explicitly unresolved until a concrete model and
  tokenizer are selected; no guessed token grant is recorded.
- Tool, secret, network, filesystem, process, database, event, mailbox, product
  read/write and command capabilities are empty or false.
- Cancellation, expiry and supersession are terminal; late completion is
  rejected before proofreader entry.
- The deterministic proofreader is the sole egress route. Direct downstream,
  human-gate, verified-fact, approval and command-shaped output are forbidden.

## Topology conclusion

The contract is transport-neutral. Three future topologies are catalogued but
unselected:

- in-cell local model;
- host-brokered local model; and
- remote-provider broker.

The least authority-widening decision now is to avoid selecting by convenience.
Each topology has different provenance, licence, device, IPC/network, secret,
privacy, jurisdiction, cost, retention and failure questions. A fresh Yuri
decision must choose one and freeze its exact threat/evidence plan.

## Adversarial evidence

Fifteen deterministic cases pass their exact expected decisions. The embedded
`ignore policy` request remains evidence data and leaves the policy digest
unchanged. The following fail closed:

- transport selection;
- a guessed token limit;
- network capability opening;
- cross-practice and cross-principal frames;
- unknown and secret-class context;
- stale availability;
- oversized input and excessive draft count;
- completion after cancellation;
- proofreader bypass; and
- command-shaped output authority.

This proves protocol separation and deterministic admission checks. It does not
prove that any particular model resists prompt injection.

## Dry-run manifests

Six source-hashed manifests cover admission posture, context, topology,
resources, cancellation and egress. Every manifest is default-deny with
`dry_run: true` and `execution_enabled: false`. They contain no model, provider,
endpoint, image, credential, secret or command coordinate.
