## Contract

Installed Word and Word Online remain independent authority partitions. A
surface cookie used against the other surface, a swapped CSRF header, a result
nonce supplied for the other surface, and a consumed nonce replay must all fail
closed. Rejected combinations must cause zero product reads and reveal no raw
identifier or opaque value in durable evidence.

## Acceptance

The exact four-case matrix returns only bounded 400/401/403/409 outcomes,
records one rejected and one replayed nonce reason, performs zero product reads,
and removes the disposable database, four roles and pools.

## Limits

This is local authored-synthetic replay evidence, not a distributed session,
browser-vendor, organisational Office or production-security claim.
