## What was proved

- One fixed canonical tape covers eight independent scenarios and 53 ordered
  transitions under hard scenario and step ceilings.
- Every attempt, draft, verifier case, retry trace, grant and supersession
  coordinate resolves into the unchanged accepted Bounded Cognitive Work Cell
  protocol.
- The runner calls the accepted proofreader and cannot override its verdict or
  disposition.
- Primary five-port work produces five in-memory verified releases and two
  inert human-gate deliveries.
- Both canonical repair variants produce two observed immutable repair
  receipts without adding a repair rule.
- Schema rejection records one bounded correction request, advances only from
  attempt 4 to accepted `retry_of` attempt 5, then verifies and routes to the
  inert human gate.
- Grounding rejection advances only from attempt 6 to attempt 7; the repeated
  failure exhausts the accepted budget and aborts one edge with no third
  attempt.
- Stale context binds only the accepted non-executing, no-data grant, advances
  through exact supersession, rejects the stale completion and stops at
  `awaiting-fresh-context` without performing a read.
- Authority rejection aborts immediately with no release or human route.
- Atomic inconsistency releases neither member and records only a bounded
  correction request; it fabricates no successor attempt.
- Every transition is sequence-bound and chained by canonical SHA-256 to the
  exact tape and accepted-protocol hashes.
- Repeated runs are byte-identical and match the committed expected evidence.
- The CLI accepts no path, exposes only `validate`, `rehearse` and `trace`,
  writes no file and prints fixed labels plus aggregate counts.
