# Reception One Focused Review Context

Date: 2026-07-20

Status: `provisional_product_name_and_review_context_no_rename_authority`

Source: Yuri, in discussion after acceptance of the provider-free live-local
functional meta-grid integration

## 1. Decision recorded

**Reception One** is the leading provisional user-facing name for the fluid
Diary system. **Meta-grid** remains part of the product's vocabulary and the
preferred architectural term for the projection grammar and reconfigurable
visual surface.

This is deliberately not a choice between the two terms:

- **Reception One** can name the system and the continuity of one live Diary;
- **meta-grid** can name how that Diary is projected into many precise,
  just-in-time visual forms; and
- an ordinary practitioner-by-time grid remains one useful projection and
  fallback, not the privileged definition of the Diary itself.

No source file, user-facing label, repository identifier or public brand is
renamed by this note. Final naming, visual identity and trademark action remain
later decisions.

## 2. Product meaning

The intended invariant is **many views, one Diary**.

Practitioner focus, patient timeline, availability, aligned comparison,
selection and proposal review are not separate diaries and do not create
separate truths. They are temporary, visibly scoped ways of reading the same
backend-owned Diary state. The useful physical metaphor is opening a real
diary to the page or section needed for the task at hand: attention narrows,
but the book remains whole.

Candidate explanatory language for the focused review includes:

- `Many views. One diary.`
- `The grid is one view of the diary.`
- `You are not switching diaries. You are opening the view you need.`
- `One live diary, shaped to the work in front of you.`

These are review prompts, not accepted interface copy.

## 3. Historical provenance

The name carries real reception provenance. The historical Bernie was an elder
stateswoman of the reception team and was proud to work at Reception 1. Before
concurrent-booking coordination was introduced through reception computers
polling the central database, that station was the operational source of
current truth for the day's bookings.

Reception One therefore joins two meanings:

1. the remembered physical station where reception work and Diary truth met;
   and
2. the modern architectural principle that many intent-based projections still
   belong to one authoritative Diary.

The provenance should inform the product's character without requiring users
to know the history or absorb philosophical language during routine work.

The next product work remains Yuri's focused review of the accepted live-local

1. whether Reception One feels credible as the system name;

The focused review may generate observations and candidate refinements. It does

## 5. Naming and trademark boundary

A preliminary general-web search did not reveal an obvious dominant use of the
exact term for Australian healthcare Diary software, but that is not trademark
clearance. Before material public-brand investment, the name should be checked
for exact, similar, phonetic and visually similar marks through
[Australian Trade Mark Search](https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks/search/quick),
relevant goods/services classes, ASIC business names, domains and intended
international markets. IP Australia expressly warns that preliminary search
results are not determinative.

Business-name availability and trademark rights are separate. ASIC explains
that business-name registration does not prevent similar expressions or
protect against another party's trademark; see
[ASIC's business-name guidance](https://www.asic.gov.au/for-business-and-companies/business-names/register-a-business-name/).

This note authorises no application, professional service, fee, domain purchase
or public trademark claim.
